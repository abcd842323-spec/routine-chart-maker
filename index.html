    Why a routine chart in your own language works

    Pictures first, words second

    Toddlers “read” the picture and follow the order themselves — no nagging. The words underneath quietly build reading readiness in two languages at once.

    Your home language stays alive

    Daily words in your own language — दाँत साफ़ करो, Cepíllate los dientes, பல் துலக்கு — keep your mother tongue part of everyday life for kids growing up anywhere in the world, not just a weekend lesson.

    Tick it together

    Use the weekly style and let your child tick each day. A full row of ticks by Saturday is a reward in itself — add a sticker or a high-five for a finished week.
